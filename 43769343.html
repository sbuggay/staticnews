The issue is that the people being blocked by Cloudflare have no alternative anti-DDOS or anti-bot solutions for website owners to use. One could argue that it's "not the job" of 3rd-world countries citizens to propose alternative technical solutions to Cloudflare -- that's the website owner's problem. The current tradeoff (aka "collateral damage") is that some users like you get blocked. That's a cost the website owners are willing to live with.
