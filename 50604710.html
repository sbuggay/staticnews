vanshiglobal|prev|next|

The parallel-clients-on-one-port change is the one that matters most to me. With iperf3, a shared test server needs a separate port per concurrent client — hence the systemd template with 5201/5202/5203 in this packaging — which is awkward behind firewalls and NAT. The CPU side matters too: at 10G+ I've more often measured the test tool's single-threaded CPU limit than the link, and a single flow hashing to one LACP member makes bonded links look slower than they are, so io_uring/zerocopy plus many workers is a real fix, not just a nice-to-have.

One question the post doesn't answer: does fireperf do UDP tests with jitter/loss reporting like iperf2/3, or is it TCP-only for now? That's the main thing that keeps iperf2 around in my experience.
