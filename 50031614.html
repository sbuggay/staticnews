rociiu|prev|

Hi HN! I built Talorys, an open-source personal AI agent that runs entirely in your own Cloudflare account.

The idea came from wanting a personal AI assistant that I could own and control, without paying a monthly subscription or relying on someone else's hosted service.

Talorys uses Cloudflare Workers, Workers AI, Durable Objects, and Pages to provide:

AI chat with persistent memory and conversation history Task, note, and project management Scheduled reminders and recurring automations A responsive web interface accessible from any device Password-protected access with no signup or external accounts One-command deployment: npx create-talorys@latest Everything runs in your Cloudflare account. Your data stays under your control, and there's no telemetry or third-party backend operated by me.

It's designed to work within Cloudflare's free-tier limits for personal use. You don't need to manage a VPS, database server, or Docker containers.

The biggest challenge was making deployment simple enough that someone could set up their own AI assistant without manually configuring Cloudflare services.

The project is still early, and I'd love feedback from the HN community, especially on the architecture, security, and what would make a self-hosted personal AI agent genuinely useful.

GitHub: https://github.com/rociiu/talorys

It's MIT licensed. Contributions and suggestions are welcome!
