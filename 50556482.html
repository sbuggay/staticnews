When you are trying to get clarity on your own thoughts or making plans for your next few weeks of travel etc, do you create slides to discuss it with yourself? It is most likely a text file with minimal, but very clear points. That's because you are the closest person to yourself, having full context. People around you are not complete strangers. They have varying level of context about what you are talking about.

If you have to spend so much on whether to have slides or not, or what should be in place of them, new AI strategies etc, there is fundamentally something wrong with your audience context.

Who is your audience? some strangers who have no context of what you are talking about? Some buyers with cash who want to be impressed by the colors, themes and animations in your slides?

For most of my work situations, it is neither. It is a handful of people who already have the full context and just need to align on a few things. So in most cases, my slides boil down to a text file with short lines.

In some social groups (or gangster movies), the boss doesn't even need to say words. Just the looks and gestures are enough to interpret the orders and align everyone into a single goal.
