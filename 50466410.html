As per the comments from Ken Jin (CPython JIT developer) on the article (at the bottom of the page), the way yk is integrated with CPython isn't really that "external" though: they make use of the existing CPython JIT's tracer, micro optimizations, etc., basically only replacing the copy-and-patch backend. So yk will have to maintain the whole CPython JIT patch (sans copy-and-patch) if they want to retain the same performance after the removal of the JIT compiler from CPython[1].

[1] https://lwn.net/Articles/1102102/
