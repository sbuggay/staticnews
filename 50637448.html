> I solve 79 × 123 by entering the first operand and then turning the crank three times to load 79 × 3 into the accumulator. Next, I shift the accumulator carriage one decimal digit to the right and crank twice; because the accumulator is offset from the input register, this has the same effect as cranking twenty times in the original position; in other words, I add 79 × 20. Finally, I shift the carriage once more and complete one more turn to add 79 × 100.

People who used these sorts of calulators probably retained a better intuitive understanding of the operations than people who use modern digital calculators.
