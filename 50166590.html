> Rather, introducing even more potential implicitness on every `.` seems like it would make the language harder to learn.

The problem is that built-in pointers (references and box) are already special cased in the compiler to Just Work (tm), so this creates confusion when switching between those builtins and third-party pointers, or even stdlib pointers which aren’t special cased by the compiler.

So this is less about “potential implicitness on every .” and more about making places more first-class, and non-builtin smart pointers (both first and third class) being better able to express their semantics to the compiler, to lift every pointer to the ergonomic capabilities of builtins.
