aftbit|root|parent|prev|next|

Securely networking electrical grids offers improvements in reliability, which translates directly to safety. If the power goes off on a hot summer afternoon, people die. Networking enables faults to be detected and resolved faster, reducing the amount of time that the system stays in dangerous conditions.

Similarly, (securely) networking water treatment plants enables people to remotely verify that settings are where they're supposed to be and adjust them to match changing sample reports or intake conditions, reducing the risk of over-treating or under-treating the water, both of which can have safety consequences.

On top of that, networking enables much greater efficiency. Rather than having a person sitting at every substation ready to throw a breaker, or more likely, ready to drive there (taking time), you can have them sitting in a control room that monitors the whole grid at once.

Much of modern life would be much more expensive and much less safe without networking.
