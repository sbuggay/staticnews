It's pretty obvious that Gemini 4 Carbon is not token efficient. It also has relatively low tokens-per-second compared to other models. I can think of several reasons why this is the case:

1. Google has huge amounts of cash and compute. Their goal was to train a model that that has the highest quality & frontier performance, not create a model that's efficient to run and serve. They have other models for that, like Hydrogen.

2. Google created Carbon without caring for inference costs. DeepMind is subsidized by the rest of Google's empire. Selling inference is an insignificant part of Google's revenue. But for Anthropic and OpenAI, selling inference is their entire business model. Anthropic and OpenAI have enormous incentive to sell as much inference as they can in the most efficient manner. Google doesn't have this incentive.

3. They are one of the few companies that can be inefficient. Out of all the frontier labs, Google DeepMind is probably the least constrained by compute. Chinese labs have around 10 to 100 times less compute, so they must release highly optimized models.
