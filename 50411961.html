armcat|prev|next|

Something to keep in mind with aggressive quantisation, is that it's been empirically proven [1] that (1) quantisation has non-linear performance behaviour, and (2) generally the peak size-to-performance ratio occurs at 4-bit; anything lower and you will see significant reduction in performance.

Also, I've been playing with speech models for the past 2 years, and for me anything below 8-bit is a huge drop in quality; so quantisation for LLMs and for other bespoke modality models, is not 1:1.

[1] "Reliability Scaling Laws for Quantized Large Language Models", TUM, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.10855
