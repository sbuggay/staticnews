Cool. This looks fun and I believe you had fun playing with it.

I’ve been having fun with miniature 3D Isometric maps for a while, of course, with AI (Claude Code). I’m fascinated by the work of https://miniworld.marceloeduardo.com which I stumbled on as I was tinkering with a few of my ideas.

His Los Angeles map[1] popped up and I have a soft corner for that city and it led me into a rabbit hole.

Unlike the US, UK, and European region, India do not have 3D (no, Google do not have them). I also wanted to use only open data, nothing to call a third-party service, no photographs, no satellite data/imagery, and be able to deploy on Cloudflare Stack with the least money ever to spent except my Anthropic Tokens.

I wanted the geography to be as close to real as possible as most of them are stationery; roads, railways, the metro, lakes, building footprints, and most building heights.

Everything is pretty much drawn with AI Codes, so I don’t have to re-render anything when the data changes or updates. I scheduled the map tile creation on a MacBook Pro M1 only when I slept, so it took a few days but I like what I saw. I will, hopefully, find it intersting enough to keep tinkering with them.

The first thing I did was re-discover hometown[2] as I have no recollection or are not good with where the places are. In that similar style but where I can afford to add rotation more info/date along multiple axis - a short 3D view of walk-way along London's South Bank.[3]

1. https://miniworld.marceloeduardo.com/cidades/?city=los_angel...

2. https://manipur.oinam.com/

3. https://brajeshwar.com/misc/london-southbank/
