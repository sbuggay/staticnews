irishcoffee|root|parent|next|

> I am curious about how the prediction markets are handling this

You use funds to make bets and the lines move over time based on how the funds are distributed. I suppose another way of looking at it is like options "trading" in the US markets. You gain or lose alpha? over time relative to when you made the "trade"

Betting on things early with long odds that become obvious time is kind of the whole point. If your thought is to some how "make this fair" you're working towards some sort of market where everything is 100% efficient, everyone wins and loses the exact same amount over N days/weeks/months.

So the short answer is, put your money where your mouth is, which is why prediction markets "work" and narrow in closer to the actual outcome over time as the deadline approaches. The long answer is, without another dimension, such as money, no there isn't a way to "make this fair" at all.

Neat site though.
