deepsquirrelnet|root|parent|next|

Yep exactly! Streaming models have to run faster, because they're more compute heavy. Some compute from every batch is wasted when the next set of frames come in. Streaming models also have higher WER than fully offline models.

Our models are more comparable to nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming in that regard and I think the medium/large sizes do perform quite favorably. For offline models, I don't think it's worthwhile for us to try and compete with parakeet v2/3. Those are already really good, and we're in the process of adding those to FUTO Voice.

These models are filling some gaps we see for the kinds of things we tend to work on. But broadly they can slot into a lot of places where there isn't accelerated compute (Raspberry pi 4, laptops, local client side transcription, etc).

nodja|parent|next|

If you want really accurate timestamps, the current state of the art is to ask claude to implement forced alignment using a CTC transcription model. It uses the logprobs of a character based transcription model to find the best matching overlap. I've used this technique to generate sync files for epub/audiobooks and it's really good even when the data is dirty.

This method aligns the transcript of a 60 hour book in 20 minutes on my GPU using Parakeet CTC 0.6B which is english only, but there's similarly sized models in other languages, the models don't need to be that good at transcribing to generate good alignments.

Wow, Voxtral 3 sounds so much more artificial and “AI” as compared to the old Voxtral 1 in the comparison clips on that page! I absolutely hated listening to the new one.

Also, comparisons with Whisper always really tick me off. It’s not a dictation model so much as it is a transcription model. It’s really the only one you can use to capture incidental audio rather than one or more persons deliberately speaking into a microphone, e.g. the only one you can use to transcribe audio from surveillance footage or background audio recorders; it has a higher WER but it actually tries to capture everything (leading quite often to hallucination, unfortunately) whereas the other models they compare it against tune out background noise and dialog altogether, in my fairly extensive testing.
