Hey everyone,

If you’re a fan of the Kindle Scribe and need a better way to organize your week, check out tools.docgenie.co.uk! This simple tool allows you to create personalized weekly planner templates that can be sent to your Kindle Scribe.

You can customize your planner by choosing dates, style, layout, and even your starting date. It’s an intuitive, straightforward solution for staying organized while keeping everything synced to the cloud for easy access.

Features:

• Easy-to-use planner creator

• Syncs directly with your Kindle Scribe

• Customizable layouts and dates

• Export your plans to PDF
