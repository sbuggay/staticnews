thinkingemote|parent|prev|next|

Most "satellite" imagery on Google Maps etc is actually stitched from photographs taken from cameras attached to aeroplanes.

There are a few high resolution satellites but there frame is very small and not suited for complete coverage. If they are geostationary they cant look anywhere, or they have to look at an angle giving oblique photos. If they are moving then they are only over the part of the earth once per several days (weeks/months?)

So while these images of the landers are from a satellite orbiting the moon, the satellite is orbiting eccentric polar orbit, and the images it takes may be perfect for it's mission but might not be good enough to identify small 1960's landers.
