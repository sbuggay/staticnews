> default opt-out

The term you're looking for is "opt in"

opt-out means "we assume you want telemetry turned on. you have to disable it yourself"

"Opt" here means "to choose" so "opt out" means "you [must] choose 'out' [because it's not pre-set that way]"

"Default opt-[anything]" is meaningless because "opt" is the user choosing something, and "default" is the non-user choosing something. They're terms in opposition to each other.

It's like saying "this car is default configured with your choices." It's not possible, because you're describing something other than a default.
