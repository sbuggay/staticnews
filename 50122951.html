There is no longer anyone to turn to if this system breaks by, eg, having a critical bug that no one can figure out. You are the owner of a recovered alien spacecraft with tech you have no hope of understanding.

The new trust model is: We trust Claude to always understand the system enough to debug anything or make any reasonable change we could want. The risk might be worth the reward. But it's worth explicitly laying out the deal we're making.
