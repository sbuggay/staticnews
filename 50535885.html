Yeah, there is a limit to how much you can get from capturing reasoning traces. (Traditional distillation of weights is not possible via the API.)

Patrick Toulme noted a while ago that the biggest advantage of that in his opinion came from bootstrapping the post-training process: https://xxcancel.com/PatrickToulme/status/206921157543762774...

At Thinking Machines, they explicitly said that they used open-weights models for bootstrapping post-training, including Kimi K2.5: https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/

Speaking of Thinking Machines, that's another data point. Their Inkling Small 2 model is just a bit behind DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 in benchmark, and in my tests, that seems to be accurate, but mostly because they used less training data for pre-training, so they have less of a knowledge base, while similar reasoning capabilities. (One of the most interesting parts of Inkling Small 2 is that their training must have been very cost-efficient, apparently using less, but higher quality training data.)

And we're now seeing more and more Western near-frontier models from labs other than Anthropic and OpenAI to question whether distilling reasoning traces is really much of a difference maker.

Personally, my theory is that the gap is primarily due to Chinese labs having a harder time getting the necessary compute (especially top of the line GPUs) for training. These are, as far as I can tell, orders of magnitude differences in availability, so it wouldn't be surprising that a large compute gap is reflected in capabilities (and also indirectly visible through model sizes).

> Indication for this are for example that Chinese models think they are another model: "chinese model think its claude".

There is evidence that some Chinese labs have been training on frontier model CoT (in particular from the analysis of reasoning traces [1]), but what models claim about their identity is pretty much worthless as evidence. LLMs are not self-aware. These claims come from whatever is in their training data, and Claude/GPT output including such statements is all over the public web, including in some huggingface data dumps. Consider that people got Opus claiming to be Qwen or DeepSeek when asked in Chinese, and you'll see how weak this particular evidence is.
