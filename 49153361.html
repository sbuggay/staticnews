Dear author:

Do NOT ship your first draft! This writing is a disaster because you let an LLM go ham. It's full of writing smells and you're doing a disservice to humanity by leaving such important information typed up so awfully.

For instance. "This matters" is a writing smell. You've heard of code smells - hints that something is off about the code? Can't quite tell something is wrong? Same goes for bad writing.

If you have to say a thing matters you've made TWO MISTAKES. One, you left in the other things that don't matter and necessitate an assertion that some specific thing matters - and two, you left in the assertion that something matters! Delete them both. Gone! Poof! See how it reads afterwards. Spend those 10 words elsewhere with a better payoff.

"This matters" is a writing smell.

And stop shipping your first draft!
