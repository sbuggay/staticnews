I use iStat Menus for this. An excellent series of tools that lets you monitor just about everything that can be monitored on your Mac.

App Tamer will help you keep unruly apps under control, forcing them to run on efficiency cores instead of performance cores, slowing them down if they eat up more than X% of your CPU, stopping them entirely if they are in the background, and so much more.

The combo of iStat Menus and App Tamer is what makes it tolerable for me to have a MacBook Air as the primary laptop I use these days.

bradknowles|root|parent|next|

For me, iStat Menus is what provides the visibility into what macOS is doing at any point in time, including capacity and traffic. That’s battery, GPU, CPU, memory, Disk, Network, sensors, etc….

For enhanced automated management of the CPU, what program gets assigned to what type of CPU core, when a program might be slowed down or stopped, etc…. that’s where App Tamer comes in.

IMO, you need both monitoring and management.

App Tamer can do only so much in terms of monitoring what’s going on, and the control it gives you is limited to things that relate to CPU cores. It can’t apply the same kinds of controls to programs that are using too much memory, or causing too much disk utilization, or using too much network, etc….

But App Tamer can do a pretty good job of managing how your CPU cores get used by the programs on your system.
