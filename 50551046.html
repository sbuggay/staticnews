Starting to see more promising examples of companies post-training. From my evals [1], I've tested 3 so far:

1. Fireworks' Ember-1 (postrained on Kimi K3, improved score by 10% in my evals)

2. Particle AI's Particle-1 (fine-tuned on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, also a 10% improvement and a bit cheaper and faster)

3. Nex AGI's Nex-N2.6 (post-trained on GLM 5.3 Flash. Didn't show an improvement in my eval, and had a couple runaway loops that made it expensive).

With Thinking Machines' western open model release (Inkling Small) a few weeks ago, I expect we'll see more of this, especially out of western companies.
