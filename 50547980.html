JoshTriplett|parent|next|

I use Zulip every day, and it works incredibly well. There are still rough edges, but it's improving regularly.

The model of having threads within channels provides great organizational help. But also, these days, if you want to have a "general chat" in a channel that isn't in a thread, you can optionally do that. (I'd recommend not jumping to that just because you're coming from Slack/Discord and not accustomed to threads; give yourself some time to try them. But it can be useful for a subset of channels; some people like it or casual/social channels, for instance. I personally like threads for those too, though.)

Also, the integration and API are easy, and bots and similar are easy to write. You can also arrange to have emails turn into posts on a channel.

Despite what the site says, you don't have to self-host. The software is Open Source, and you absolutely can if you want to. But you can also use the hosted version, either free or paid, and save yourself the trouble, knowing that you can always move to self-hosted later.

I use the app on mobile, but just use the web version on desktop, in a pinned tab.

(Disclaimer: I'm on the board of the Zulip Foundation.)
