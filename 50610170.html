Tangential fun fact, this is why you don't want too many fines (pebbles/dirt/sand) going through a crusher (eg. jaw or cone crusher, the kind that makes big rocks into small rocks). The wear liners are made of manganese steel, and need to be munching on big rocks to maintain their hardness. Tip too much fine material through there and the work-hardened surface wears away, after which the non-work-hardened material underneath will wear much faster.
