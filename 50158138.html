Note that OpenBSD is on 7.9 now and all releases are possible breaking changes.

They release every six month and increment by 0.1. And after 0.9 they rollover to the next whole number.

So don’t think of version “8.0” as a major release per se, since OpenBSD is a fixed schedule release - not a Major.Minor release.
