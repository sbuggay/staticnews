It's being called a data diode (diodes usually allows current in one direction only).

It can be implemented with a fiber optics cable with only the transmitter on the secure side, a laser+photo cell for real air gap, cutting the rx cable in a rs232 or having a microcontroller with an output pin connected to another controllers input pin. It's used to monitor for example industrial systems and power plants.
