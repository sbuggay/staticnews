My team ran on-premise with Talos for the last bunch of years. It avoided tons of odd issues I saw when I built k8s clusters on top of Ubuntu that I never quite ironed out.

It gave me a ton of appreciation for Kubernetes and Linux and how k8s is built on linux assumptions: the file system layout’s a little different in Talos to the default k8s assumptions, so there were some small adjustments I’d make every now and then.

Sidero’s professional support is out of the world, too. I had simple questions and got answers back from their devops engineers who are running clusters 100-1000 times the size of anything I had to touch, or from their software engineering team who write Talos.

Definitely worth a look if you’re hosting in your home lab, definitely worth a look if you’re doing self-hosted k8s for your job.
