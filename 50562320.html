D_R_Farrell|parent|next|

This is definitely an interesting observation. And because you are working on the screen itself, you may not experience as much drift. The best way to be precise in your requests is to make the request on the explicit element itself, which Onlook makes it quite easy to do with our inline chat.

What that essentially means is that you can click an element on a frame and a little chat bubble will appear right above it for you to specifically target that element. And then depending on the blast radius of what the agent assumes you're trying to do with your prompt it will either change that specific element or elements around it.

This of course assumes that you are using a decent model level. And so I generally like to work with Claude Opus on low thinking to have the right balance of impact and speed.
