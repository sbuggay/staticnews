Fair on the 60-second line — that was a bad generalisation. Ad density is channel-dependent; I should have said that in the README (fixing it).

The extension is not an ad blocker, it is a punisher: it detects the ad overlay before the skip button appears, grabs your exact playback position, and reloads with the timestamp so YouTube seeks back to your spot. That is the whole trick — you never sit through the ad and you do not lose your place. There is a cooldown so it cannot reload-loop, and a local ad-history popup so you can see what it caught.
