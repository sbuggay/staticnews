alirezamshi|parent|next|

Thanks for your comment. We have also tried it ourselves in relbench (Stanford's predictive modelling on relational databases), and surpassed tabular foundational models: https://github.com/Leeroo-AI/kapso/tree/main/benchmarks/relb...

Our hypothesis is that similar to RL principle, you can experiment in the environment (predictive modelling on relational databases), learn from it, and compound your domain harness with these bank of lessons, instead of re-training foundational table model, which puts these learnings in model weights. This enables faster iteration, and transparent learning over time.

As a proof for this domain, Kapso leads in classification, regression and recommendation tasks vs. best tabular foundational model (Kumo AI/Nvidia): https://star-project.stanford.edu/relbench/leaderboard/#clas...
