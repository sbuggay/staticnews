Chandangalani|root|parent|

Virtual card services cover part of this. Privacy.com in the US, or the virtual cards from Ramp and Brex, let you set a per-card limit by transaction, month or lifetime, and lock a card to one merchant. The catch is that the card only knows the amount and the merchant. It can't tell whether this particular charge is the one you actually meant to allow.

The "payment key" you describe works best as a layer between the agent and the money. Every payment request is checked against daily count and amount limits plus a delegated budget. Anything over the limit is blocked outright, not sent to you for a yes. Anything you've flagged gets a one-time approval tied to that exact amount and payee, so a "yes" can't be reused for a different charge.

Disclosure: I build this at PrivateVault (privatevault.ai). Happy to share how we structure the limits if that's useful.
