deathanatos|prev|next|

Can we adjust the URL to have a final trailing slash? The HN URL points to,

  https://www.thomas-huehn.com/knuth-reward-check
But this means the images, whose hrefs are like,

  src="./bug-report-knuth-2006.jpg"
Don't load; that relative-ref results in a URL of

  https://www.thomas-huehn.com/bug-report-knuth-2006.jpg
Which is a 404.

One can infer that the post's URI is like a "directory" of the subresources for the post, including those images; the URL of

  https://www.thomas-huehn.com/knuth-reward-check/
(n.b. the trailing slash) adjusts the resolution of the image's relative-ref now to:

  https://www.thomas-huehn.com/knuth-reward-check/bug-report-knuth-2006.jpg
And the images load. Yes, trailing slashes in URLs are significant in the case of resolving a relative ref like that.

If the author is here, you might consider redirecting the non-slash to the slash.
