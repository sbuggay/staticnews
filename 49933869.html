>important difference is that now Apple has some of the best CPUs in the world.

Apple Silicon has the "best CPU" in specific dimensions of low power usage and single-core performance with up to 512GB RAM ... but not in multi-core workloads with up to 4TB of RAM.

Apple optimizing the cpu design for the best power efficiency and single-core speed is the right tradeoff when the target products are consumer devices that run on batteries such such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops.

In contrast, the Intel Xeon, AMD Threadripper, Amazon Graviton cpus will beat Apple Silicon on heavy multi-core workloads because those chips are optimized for server utilization. The tradeoff is they're not suitable for personal consumer devices.

The rumours of a potential future Apple M8 + NVIDIA seems to be targeting AI inference tasks rather than general enterprise server workloads. Maybe because of Apple's unified memory architecture has faster RAM bandwidth than Xeon/Threadripper which is important for generating tokens/sec.
