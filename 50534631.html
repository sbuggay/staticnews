There's a major misunderstanding of what this article is saying going on here, and to be fair the article doesn't explain it very well.

The 104B number is the external revenue of NVIDIA's Singaporean subsidiary in 2025. This includes any orders for GPU chips that happen to get routed through the Singapore entity. For those unaware, end customers generally don't buy anything from NVIDIA. Customers by NVIDIA GPUs through a large network of OEMs/ODMs and resellers. Many of the largest companies in this network are based in east asia (eg. Wistron, Foxconn, Asus, Lenovo) and when your local hyperscaler makes a server order, one of these OEMs places an order to NVIDIA for GPUs. Because of this much of this revenue likely comes from end customers all over the world, including the US.

In fact, in one of the 2025 earnings calls NVIDIA stated that 99% of the singapore entity's revenue for export-restricted chips came from the US.

It's a little bit difficult to untangle exactly how much of NVIDIA's revenue came from end customers in Singapore in any given year. In 2025 it looks to be about 19B. That might be unreasonable for a country singapore's size, but it's certainly not half of NVIDIA's revenue.
