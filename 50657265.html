Early in 2025 a bunch of folks realized that AMD made a bunch of cards with mediocre compute but incredibly fast HBM2 memory in their circa 2018 gfx906 datacenter series, so you could get 32GB VRAM capable of incredible 1 TB / s bandwidth for $200 (the closest in today’s cards is north of $3000)

There has been a rich little ecosystem of folks optimizing llama.cpp for these odd cards (no tensor cores, no native bf16 etc.) including my little personal repo https://github.com/kinchahoy/llama-of-vega.cpp. Stuff like this focused on more modern RDNA doesn’t help us at all.

It’s been interesting to see how much each new frontier model can figure out in terms of optimizing for these GPUs and what they can’t!

Fable and Astra will regularly run out of ideas until I point them to someone’s actually thoughtful repo and then they’ll diligently incorporate ideas.

The optimization wins are huge (I was initially skeptical that per model kernel optimizations would be worth much). 27B at a pristine Q8 quant goes from base llama.cpp at some 250 pp / 25 tg to 600 pp / 40 tg even before speculative decoding.
