It seems to really be a nice step-up and is getting quite close to the frontier. I wish they'd start focusing on the reasoning efficiency now, though. I have a simple (relatively) test task: writing a simple math evaluator library in Nim that I test some LLMs with, and GLM 5.2 spent over 15 minutes (!) reasoning, spending about 45k tokens, before it finally wrote the file.

I know it's hard but now that their models are good enough, I think this should become a higher priority task.

Currently on https://artificialanalysis.ai/#output-tokens GPT 5.5 xhigh spends 16k tokens total on average, GPT 5.5 high is 10k, GLM 5.2 is 42k (!).
