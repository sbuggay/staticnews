Everyone goes for the blue domes.

The real historic sites can be seen via

  - taking a boat tour to the dead volcano "Caldera"
  - visiting the Prehistoric ruins at Akrotiri (south side of the island)
The weirdest thing about this island is that everything is built on a caldera rim, much higher than sea level. Extremely unusual for an island.

The museum of prehistoric Thera is also worth a visit if you make it there! It is fairly small but contains some incredibly well-preserved artifacts. The frescoes are stunning.

I found Fira (nearby "large" town on Santorini, very close to the sites) miserably crowded, but seeing Thera was very cool. This is all about 1500 years older than Pompeii, so a very different experience IMO.
