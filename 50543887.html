The strategic commander always felt like it should have been more successful. On paper it is the perfect the missing half for the native PC FPS controller.

So, the mouse, If you had asked anyone to design a bespoke FPS controller, they would not design a mouse, yet somehow, this thing built to do something totally different, point at things on the screen, has proven to be the best fps controller, nobody has come up with anything better. Probably something about it's huge(for controller sizes) swing distance and absolute positioning.

The problem is the other half, the keyboard, on the one hand it is sort of nice to have 100+ buttons to use, but you almost never need that many and it stinks to only have binary(on/off) movement.

I have seen half controllers designed to solve this, but they never have enough buttons and are hard to hold, but the strategic commander, that makes one think, forget what it was built for, the RTS, it looks awkward as hell for that, but it does look perfect for the FPS.
