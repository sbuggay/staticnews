I use a little trick. Focus on your screen at the midpoint of the two asteroids. Place your pointer finger, pointing up, a short distance away from your nose in the direction of the screen. You'll see two copies of your finger since you aren't looking at it. Move your finger until the two copies of it are pointing at the same location on the two copies of the asteroid. Now, without moving your finger, look at it. The two asteroids will become three, with a new one in the middle. While keeping your eye positions locked on your finger, shift your focus to the third asteroid.

If you do this, different distances to the screen can make the trick a little easier or harder, but you should be able to dial it it any basically any distance.
