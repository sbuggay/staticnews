Roughly 1oz every shot adds up quick when it’s a busy range. Roughly a lb per person per round.

It’s quite dense and usually chemically non-reactive, so it doesn’t take up much space and is easy to ignore.

That and selling range brass for recycling made a big dent in the costs of running the place.

Zero measurable runoff or ground infiltration, too.
