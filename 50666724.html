To get to Arcosanti you get off I-17 at Cordes Junction. Normally people get off at Cordes to get gas, use the restroom, visit the supercharger, or to go west to Prescott. There's no mining, timber, farming or any other reason why people usually set up a town.

Soleri built in this area because it was half way between Phoenix and Flagstaff. It's unincorporated, so he could do just about whatever he wanted without having to deal with regulators.

Cordes Junction is... barren. It's high desert, so there's no cactus. It's in the rain shadow of the mountains around Prescott. The libraries have free tickets to go visit. I'm not interested.

I took my passenger [0] to Cordes Junction, where he had a free room to stay. He didn't do well there. His bail was much higher that time - he got his aunt to bail him out. His sister came out to rescue his cats. She provided the backstory for how he'd fallen down when he was ~18/19/20 years old.

There are some little music festivals that play at Arcosanti.
