seizethecheese|root|parent|next|

> What makes something an "early vote"

When you run agents in a battle, one always finishes first. We let users vote for the finished agent without waiting for the agent that's still working. This is contrary to typical Arena methodology (like Arena.ai) but I think it's appropriate for agents since they can take dramatically different amounts of time to finish tasks.

This is why DeepSeek 4.1 Flash is doing so well on our leaderboard, it's super fast and often sufficient.

> and a "developer vote"

This is confusing, sorry. Inspecting our data it's almost entirely people doing software developments with agents. It's just a vote.

> (Also, out of curiosity, how are you funding this free inference for frontier models Is this not massively abuseable?)

This is a pivot from Bottomless (YC W19). That business is doing okay and funding our runway, in addition to fundraising back in 2021.
