Metabolism First is a tantalizing prospect but as yet has never succeeded in actually making any useful compounds. Cycling CO2 and H2 into formate and pyruvate which are then destroyed by the same conditions is insufficient to claim this could act as a compelling alternative to Genetics First. Metabolism must be more than cycling reactions - it must be genetically encoded reaction CONTROL!
