> libjxl's maximum effort is very slow. It produces files 2–7% smaller than its default for photos, and ~25% smaller for the screenshot and illustration, but takes 9–46 seconds, which is 50–180x as long as its default. jixel gets the same results in a couple of seconds.

libjxl's slow speed at efforts >7 is primarily due to an iterative evaluation-optimization loop of a perceptual error metric called Butteraugli (by tuning quantization weights). This, as well as some other computations in libjxl encoding, happen to be data-parallel and run faster on GPUs than multicore CPUs.

In case anyone is interested, I've been working on a GPU-accelerated libjxl port (supporting CUDA and Metal for now) that makes this much faster, closer to libjxl's normal speeds: https://github.com/yunho-c/gjxl - geared for encoding a large batch images into JPEG XL. It's also faster by around 2.9x at normal effort (7).
