Back in 2005, I was working a job at a small-town newspaper in a town I’d never lived before. That blog was called Turking.com, and it seemed like it was going well for a bit. We even got a mention on the AWS website. (The domain most assuredly was bought by Amazon and is managed by MarkMonitor.)

I didn’t get a ton of money from the deal, but I did get enough to pay for a new laptop. I had some regrets about selling it (in part because I originally built the site with someone else), but I was in a bit of a dire financial situation which that proved to be the starting point for getting myself out of.

That domain purchase was notably more than I ever made from clicking and classifying random pictures, that said.
