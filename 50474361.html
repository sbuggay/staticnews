nseggs|parent|next|

Pretty much. You need an Organization developer account, not an Individual account to get the PCC entitlement, but after that it’s pretty easy. I’ve been using it in TestFlight and now a shipping app since WWDC and it’s pretty solid. A lot more performant than I had expected. Context windows could be a bit larger (though it’s much better than the on-device model). PCC usage is allotted to the end users Apple ID or iCloud account , not the app or my development account, which makes it possible to ship a lightweight app and not worry about my own server costs. Some of the background gotchas and PCC rate limiting behaviors are obnoxious but I’ll take what I can get. Some of it is just standard iOS behavior.
