m132|root|parent|next|

PAC is a standard Armv8.3 extension and has been implemented in a lot of processors outside of the Apple ecosystem [1]. Lots of PAC instructions use HINT space encodings, which makes them NOPs on unsupported processors, and those that don't will just trap into the kernel, making it possible to simply skip or emulate them.

A great deal of AArch64 Linux distros already builds binary packages exclusively with PAC, and those continue to run on SoCs that lack support, like that of the Raspberry Pi.

[1] https://gpages.juszkiewicz.com.pl/arm-socs-table/arm-socs.ht...
