It's funny how hard it is to create a sizable text corpus that you are pretty sure is not contaminated by text from the internet. It turns out that using a company's private codebases is one of the most reliable ways to do it.

The openai blog also doesn't make clear how much increasing context length of the models plays a role in lowering perplexity. Perplexity is higher at the start of the context window when the model doesn't have enough context to know whats going on yet. The longer the window, the more easy to predict tokens it has to average away the high cost of the first few. Context lengths have gotten much longer since gpt3.5...
