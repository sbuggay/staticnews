Some of the games created for Bored.com appear to be clones of other games on the site, which is a bit strange to me.

"Chat Pester" [1] is similar to R74n's "RageBait Simulator" [2], "Bottomless" [3] is similar to Neal Agarwal's "The Deep Sea" [4], and "Spend It All" [5] is similar to Neal Agarwal's "Spend Bill Gates' Money" [6]. "Daily word" [7] is nearly identical to Josh Wardle's "Wordle" [8].

They have tweaked these games somewhat (except for "Daily word"), but since the point of the site is to be a link aggregator, I am not particularly impressed by Chat Pester being ranked much higher than its source material [9] that used to be ranked #2 [10].
