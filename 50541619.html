gilleain|root|parent|next|

Perhaps. They explain why:

> Shuffling keeps the letters and destroys their order, so the difference in accuracy measures what the model knows about the sequence itself.

So by analogy, if you know a word in English starts with 'Q', you could reasonably predict the next letter would be 'u'. By shuffling, you might encounter the letter Q later on, and not successfully predict a 'u'.

Put another way, the shuffled sequences can only use frequency analysis for prediction, while the unshuffled ones have local and global order signals.
