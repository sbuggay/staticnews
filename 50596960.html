The matrix approach for calculating the DFT also gives us a nice way to proof that the DFT can always be inverted. We just need to show that matrix F is always (for any positive integer N) invertible.

That can be proved in a number of ways. E.g. showing that det(F)!=0 or showing linear independence of the columns of F is sufficient.
