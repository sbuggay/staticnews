(A fireworks show and a drone light show are both visual spectacles in the night sky, and they are each complex in their own right, but they are nevertheless different things.)

It turns out that for various linguistic and historical reasons, many humans use “I like X” as a shorthand to mean “I like the complex deeper thing entwined with and undergirding X”. This can be confusing if one is unaware of the phenomenon.
