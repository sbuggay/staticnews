I feel like this might be inherent to software and hardware development.

With hardware you pretty much get to restart on every product you make. Sure, you have many of the same (types) of components, but with every iteration, you move them around and pretty much have to rethink how every part fits together and interacts. With software you have this continuous blob that you keep adding features to. If it turns out there are bugs, not a problem, you just push an update and hope that fixes more than it breaks.
