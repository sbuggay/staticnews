Not saying that a modern browser is simpler than Doom, but still, I think you might be able to get one of those to load. Someone successfully ran Postgres, which is very large and complicated.

Edit: nested, I was able to get as far as starting the smallest package I could find on trynix.dev, I just put in nano and then loaded the earliest version I could find. Here are the steps:

1. on your own computer open a tab in Chrome or Firefox and go to unkernel.dev and wait for it to load.

2. in the shell inside unkernel type:

    chromium
This downloads and starts it within the Linux environment your tab is running! It is very fast.

3. In the nested Chromium tab (inside unkernel.dev), go to trynix.dev and look for a very small package, for example nano and scroll down to an early version, select it and click boot.

It will then boot, so now you're running unkernel within unkernel. I got this working on a 2026 Mac mini. (24 GB of RAM).
