For those not getting the scope of this. The following has been compromised for all living danish citizens and foreign nationals which have had recidence. And quite a few dead ones as well.

- Social security number

- Age

- Sex

- Family relations

- Physical address

- Protected addresses

- Sex change

This is a country with quite good health records. Unfortunately also previous problems with proper non-reversible anonymisation of said data when used for research.
