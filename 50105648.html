Hello HN,

This is Diego from Krea. Today we are announcing the open beta of Krea 3, our newest foundational image model.

In a nutshell, it’s a frontier image model in terms of quality, but at a fraction of the speed and a fraction of the price.

We will be releasing a more technical update, like we did with Krea 2 (https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-technical-report), but this website shows the capabilities we focused on:

* Native regional edits

* Precise edits so that there is minimal area degradation pixel-wise

* Frontier image quality. Benchmarks still being worked on, but in our internal evals it seems a no-brainer replacement to NBP or GPT Image, both speed and cost-wise.

* Faster edits (AFAICK, this should unlock a pareto frontier for speed in frontier edit models).

In terms of future updates, the team is thinking about letting folks fine-tune Krea 3 (via LoRAs) and open-weights, but we’re still in the middle of launch hiccups and then we’ll revisit the next iterations.

Thanks!
