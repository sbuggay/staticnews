spwa4|parent|prev|next|

RL training, the second phase of LLM training, is based on "I did X, was that good/bad?" and that 1 bit of information is the training data.

So you give the user a suggestion, and the user accepts -> good

You give the user a suggestion, and the user refuses and types something else -> bad (plus some supervisory training data)

The main performance enhancer in LLMs is getting high quality training data. So, first, any extra training data will help. Second this is training data that's directly relevant to their product, and thus higher quality than many other sources.

I'd believe any model provider is mining the shit out of every last customer interaction they can get, not just this.
