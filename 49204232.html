Improv is a very good hobby to have. It helps you to get out of difficult situations.

One of the things done at an improv show is called "story in 4".

To do a four-person improv story with random changes, have one player start a scene. Ring a bell or shout "Change!" at random times. When that happens, the last speaker must change their last line, or a new person must jump in to shift the plot, setting, or character traits completely.

It is super fun on stage and very useful in meeting rooms where you might need to pivot quickly.
