Purely outside looking in, but yes, I'd argue you are right and higher-quality is a major part of it, both for pre- and post-training. There appears to be a far greater willingness to adopt specifically tailored training sets from third-parties, consistently update and build on them.

From simple frontend work to strategically adding virtual rulers when working with LIDAR scans to extract more accurate data, there are very clear points when Anthropic introduces a new external data set into their mix and while I have seen the same from competitors such as Google, there simply appears to be a greater tendency to stick with these once they have them.

As an example, at release it was quite apparent that Gemini 3 Pro, 3 Flash and Opus 4.5 had a similar, possible even the same frontend-focused training base bought in from a third party. But while Anthropic has had both smaller and larger improvements across each subsequent Opus release, I have not noticed such a consistent improvement with Gemini models over the last year. Maybe Anthropic simply buys updates more often, maybe they create more artificial PRs on top, who knows.

Same with the LIDAR scans, there was a point a few months ago when models started to exhibit a very systematic way of handling visual references in some few leather-crafting tasks, enabling whole new ways of working more efficiently combining existing measurements with images. Step-by-step pixel measurements, compensation for angles and poor captures, etc. Have since seen this in other models, again very much feels like a bought in data set that they are pushing as far as it goes.

Then there is 3D capability with models like Muse Wonder Full.
