I recently got a bee in my bonnet to "fix" my Fire TV, and I'm so glad I did. It's my dream setup now: A single stock remote control that controls everything with Debian in the middle.

The trick was setting it automatically boot to HDMI1 so I never have to deal with ads or the slow UI. HDMI1 is connected directly to a mini ITX case running bare Kodi with no window manager.

I paired the remote with that machine instead of the TV. And then configured it to either forward the button presses to Kodi (if focused) or forward them back to the television via wifi (which you can do via Fire TV's "developer mode").

apaprocki|root|parent|next|

I would have thought so, but for us that is not the case. I can open it in the dark and swipe. Face unlock is instant and the swiping gestures don’t require precision. I have muscle memory of the presses required to turn off the TV via CEC in another room without seeing the OSD.

And if anyone claims it’s too “advanced”, my youngest has had no problems controlling any room from his iPad since at least age 6, if not earlier.

You could always have a dedicated iPad as a remote instead of using a phone. The point being it’s much harder to lose an iPad on the couch than a small stick remote that finds every excuse to inexplicably disappear when needed most.

The benefits of centralizing everything onto a phone that is already in the “critical path” outweigh everything else for me.
