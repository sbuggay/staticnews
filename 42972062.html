This is a core communication people issue. The problems arise because in consensus models, people are expected to adopt archetypical roles based somewhat on preference, but mostly on the needs of the group.

If no one else is doing that type of role, someone will be forced into doing it, and will adopt characteristics of that role, regardless of their personal opinions about it.

When they get punished for fulfilling the role, you lose people.

There's no way around this structure so long as you use a consensus model in whole or part.

This is basic stuff covered in your college level intro to communications coursework.
