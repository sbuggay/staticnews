I feel like this is a bit of a "draw the rest of the owl" problem. With a couple of terabytes of logs per day, god knows how many metric timeseries, thousands of cloud environments, thousands of repositories, hundreds of thousands of build pipelines running every day, it's not something you can just point an agent at and call it a day. Segmenting and correlating the data is _hard_.
