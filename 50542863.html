Just emerged from wiki rabbit hole; TIL: these lidar maps are made by having a plane do a bunch of overlapping passes (similar pattern to a lawn mower) and beaming down lidar (tiny lasers) the whole while.

A table here says the number of laser shots ('points') per square meter is up to 18 (Estonia): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_lidar_dataset

And the accuracy of the height measurements (i.e. 'altimetric accuracy') are to ~10cm! https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/lidar-data-swisstopo

The higher-resolution maps are managed by the individual states and territories. Some of them (VIC, WA, SA, TAS) make you pay, others (NSW, QLD, ACT, NT) don't. NSW has both the old raster ones [1] that you could buy print versions of, and the vector ones [2] (select "topographic" under "basemaps") available online.

[1] https://maps.six.nsw.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/public/NSW_...

[2] https://portal.spatial.nsw.gov.au/explorer/index.html
