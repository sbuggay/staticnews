I doubt we will, people want to be able to see the same documents/messages/etc from their PC and their phone.

That doesn't strictly require a central server, but a central server certainly makes any kind of sync orders of magnitude easier for everyone. That becomes a central weakness whether the client software is deployed via javascript in a web browser or packaged as a desktop app.

Running a proprietary service might buy you a small amount of time compared to web-scanners, but it doesn't change the risk model. In fact, given that the browser is still a fairly effective sandbox, moving to published desktop app probably increases the risk for clients by increasing the blast radius of any issues there.
