There is a reason why these inference wrappers exist, it’s a way to arbitrage compute.

Imagine, some team or individual has a way to access pre-emptible GPU hours at some low price.

Let’s say that low price is $6/GB300/hour.

If the inference wrapper can achieve demand of some base load of, equivalent of a rack of GB300s (72 GPUs).

Essentially, the wrapper can route baseload traffic to the primary provider at market API prices (and make no money).

But when the cheap GPUs are available, they can route them to the hosted instances to internalize the API margins.

DeepSeek’s team has an estimate of ~$36/gpu/hour of monetization for DeepSeek 4.1 flash at off peak rates.

So this team would be making $(36-6)/gpu/hour for any traffic they can route to the cheap GPUs.

In many ways, inference APIs should be validated like a trusted actor end to end.
