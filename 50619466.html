Not so simple! Rare precious objects tend to be preserved and passed on, and it can distort our historical perspective. We have the same effect in architecture where only the rare costly buildings survive and the cheap constructions disappear (allowing the "culture critics” to rant on how buildings of the past were so beautiful)

Trash dumps of course are indeed full of supposedly common things, but they are just one source.
