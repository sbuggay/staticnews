I met Mr Draper once. I was staying at the SFO hyatt, or maybe the Mariott, and had a couple hours to kill after breakfast and noticed there was some sort of conference going on, so I crashed it. I walked into a talk room and sat in the front row and introduced myself to my neighbors. On one side was Steven Levy (author of many books in the industry) and the other was Bill Draper. I don't really remember the conversation, nor even the topic of the conference - it was a brief conversation in the 5 or 10 minutes before the talk started. At the time I didn't really know who he was but I was a little star-struck by Mr Levy, and it was Levy who told me I should be more impressed by being next to Draper. Mr Draper thought that was funny and the three of us chatted a bit more about whatever. I found it interesting that Mr. Draper was a surprisingly relatable guy (particularly given what I know about him know) and that he was very insightful of technology for someone who was in his 70 or 80s at the time.

RIP Mr. Draper. I didn't know it when we talked, but you helped make my career possible - thanks!

I got to meet Bill briefly while taking part in his grandson Adam’s Boost VC accelerator in the early bitcoin days.

Bill would attend both events in support of Tim and Adam. I had a great experience with both the Drapers as a founder.

One time, while living with my cofounder in the hotel Tim was restoring in downtown San Mateo, I broke the hotel elevator.

The laundry was on the basement level and ~everyone used this brick to prop open the door so could dash to the laundry move it to the dryer and dash back without loosing the car.

Well, the brick was not well propped and somehow the door pushed it to a point where it could close but also somehow carry it up the shaft.

Watching the door close it was just like, uh oh.

Up goes the elevator idk a few stories and then the sound of a brick tumbling down the shaft hitting who knows what.

Ya, the elevator stopped working immediately. I owned up to it the next day, writing Tim and offering to pay for what probably would have consumed all of our accelerator funds of damage I had caused.

He thanked me for my honesty and said not to worry about it.

Tim (and Adam) went on to invest in not one but two of my ventures.

Anyway, I realize this is about Bill but I think a person can be reflected in not only themselves but those that they raise and wanted to share this.

Oh and Bill, at least back then, did not think much of Bitcoin. :)
